# Mesh-CNN v2 project progress

Updated 23 September 2026 after the project-wide proof-of-concept revision. This report covers the active project in `/home/s2307298/projects/FDTD_ML` on `codex/mesh-cnn-v2`. The [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) defines the entire C0–C9 roadmap; [the pilot protocol](docs/v2_pilot.md) specifies its first demonstration.

## Current status

**The current objective remains a compact demonstration on unseen engineering-like silhouettes and distributed sparse scenes.** The compiled CUDA FDTD backend, eight-scene GPU development profile, per-condition physics teacher optimizer, teacher-worker integration, training, and post-freeze evaluation paths are implemented. Model training and frozen C8/C9 target evaluation have not started. C0–C2 plus ordinary separated C3/C5 scenes form the training bridge; bounded C8/C9 scenes are reserved for evaluation.

No v2 CNN has been trained. No new teacher corpus, frozen target-scene test manifest, or C8/C9 physical evaluation has been completed. The 1,104-example historical import is available training data, not a new target-scene dataset.

The existing `launch` command still implements the superseded single-object C0–C2 design. The revised `profile-compact` and `launch-compact` paths use compiled CUDA. Eight of eight GPU development references qualified; the original six fixed teachers were weak outside one circle. A bounded 96-evaluation smooth-density search found improvement on development silhouettes, a high-contrast pair, and a PEC circle. The worker now optimizes each train/validation condition before saving three extra teacher profiles; a one-condition GPU smoke produced nine valid candidates and a readable dataset. The cost profile is being refreshed for this added search. No compact manifest or CNN campaign has been frozen or launched.

## Measured evidence and its limits

- The Cython-bound `nvcc` cooperative kernel keeps the entire FDTD time loop on GPU with zero host/device transfers during stepping. Three paired TITAN RTX benchmarks measured end-to-end wall speedups of 66.6× (96² dielectric circle), 35.8× (32² conformal PEC rectangle), and 43.4× (64² distributed dielectric), with maximum paired far-field relative L2 difference `3.01e-15` versus the legacy Torch path. Raw records: `runs_v2/cuda_fdtd_benchmark_v1/report.json`.
- The eight-scene compiled-CUDA development profile qualified all eight references and passed representative duration, quadrature, contour, and PML probes. Its original fixed six-mesh teacher set improved non-circle raw accuracy in only one of 20 development conditions, and there by 2.1%; the circle dominated the positive gate. The 96-trial optimization pilot at 48² found no gain for the low-contrast C3/C5 pairs or aircraft, but raw gains of 1.115× for the ship, 1.111× for the vehicle, and 1.149× for the PEC circle. Against independent 384-cell reference fields, ship and vehicle gains persisted at 1.051× and 1.128×. These are development results, not frozen C8 claims. Records: `runs_v2/teacher_optimization_pilot_v1/` and `runs_v2/teacher_reference_check_v1/summary.json`.
- Material-specific 48² teacher search on a lossless `εr=8` two-object development scene found a 5.80× raw score gain over uniform; its 256→384 complex-field and width changes were 0.409% and 0.361%. A lossless `εr=8` ship yielded 4.13× but its 256→384 reference change was 1.60%, so it is exploratory and cannot supply a qualified label. A lossless `εr=12` vehicle did not meet the `1e-5` tail gate at 560 ns. A separate `tanδ≈0.02` variant settled, but its reference also remains unconverged; it is not a replacement label. The conditional loss check skips lossy variants when the corresponding lossless simulation settles. Records: `runs_v2/teacher_material_pilot_v1/summary.json` and `runs_v2/teacher_material_loss_pilot_v1/conditional_summary.json`.
- Four TITAN RTX GPUs were available during the earlier profile. Its 16 shape/material rows ran in 109 seconds; only nine yielded complete compatible projections, comprising eight dielectric and one PEC row. Seven PEC examples had an incompatible tested grid. At 192 cells, four dielectric examples were unsettled after 70 ns and five PEC examples exceeded the 50,000-step profiling cap.
- Under that profile's 140 ns assumption, compatible rows projected to 18.16 elapsed hours for 128 lineages or 36.31 hours for 256 on four GPUs. These are partial projections for the superseded campaign, not complete estimates, rigorous lower bounds, or estimates for the new compact design. See `runs_v2/profile_c0_c2/profile.json` and `runs_v2/c0_c2_pilot/campaign_status.json`.
- A dielectric-circle reference smoke under the earlier strict protocol reached 512 cells per axis, with separate duration, quadrature, contour, and PML checks and maximum variation 0.1853%. This does not establish a universal reference-grid requirement. At 32 cells, star dielectric and smooth-lobed PEC CPU/CUDA far fields agreed within `5e-16` relative L2; backend agreement alone is not physical convergence.
- A controlled C4 CPU campaign covers 2/5/10/20 mm gaps (`0.0067–0.0667 λ` at 1 GHz) between two 60 mm-radius, `εr=4` dielectric circles. For all four, the 192→256 uniform-grid change is at most 0.481% complex far field and 0.430% width; five fixed total-Ez gap-line samples also pass a 5% spatial gate. Duration, quadrature, contour, and PML probe changes are at most 0.041% in the far field, 0.035% in width, and 0.115% along the gap line. The maximum recorded reference variation is 0.481%. In 100 uniform/geometry-policy runs, center/hybrid policies meet both 2% errors at 96×96 versus uniform 128×128 (43.8% fewer cells), and both 5% errors at 64×64 versus uniform 96×96 (55.6% fewer cells) for every gap. At 10% the best policies do not save cells versus uniform 64×64. The best raw-accuracy policy remains best after the time-step exponents 0, 0.02, 0.05, and 0.1 are applied. This is fixed-policy development evidence for one shared geometry/material/orientation family, not a held-out result or CNN gain; three candidate runs are unsettled. At 64×64 the selected nonuniform meshes roughly halve `dt` and double step count; CPU wall time did not fall consistently despite fewer cell updates. All 140 stored reference/probe/mesh artifacts passed finite-field, exact-axis-count, monotonicity, and grading-at-most-3 checks. Data and per-run records: `runs_v2/c4_gap_sweep_v2_near_field/c4_gap_sweep_report.json`.
- A controlled C3 CPU campaign covers equal-material dielectric circle pairs at center separations 0.20 m and 0.34 m, a 2:1 radius ratio at 0.26 m separation, and a diagonal pair at 0.21 m separation/45°. The source is 1 GHz and far-field observables span 0.8–1.2 GHz. Three references qualified at 256 cells/axis and the size-ratio reference at 384; maximum recorded uncertainty is 0.421%. Duration, quadrature, contour, and PML probes pass; maximum probe changes are 0.073% complex field and 0.064% width. All 80 uniform/interface/center/hybrid runs at 32/48/64/96/128 cells were accepted. The center policy had lower raw joint scattering loss in 19/20 matched-budget conditions, and stayed best after dt exponents 0, 0.02, 0.05, and 0.1 in those 19. At 5% field-and-width error, center saved 55.6% of cells in two scenes and matched uniform in two; at 10%, it saved 43.8% in one and 55.6% in another, with no savings in the other two. The 2% target was reached by center at 96 cells in all four, while uniform failed it at the largest tested 128 cells in two. These results show fixed-policy opportunity but not universal cell savings or learned transfer.
